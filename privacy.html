Information we collect

When you submit a vehicle sourcing request or contact us, we may process your name, company, email, phone number, country, buyer type, vehicle requirements, destination, budget information and other details you choose to provide.

Why we process it

We use the information to review and respond to your sourcing request, communicate with you, assess possible supply options, coordinate a potential transaction and keep necessary business records.

Legal basis

Processing is based on taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performance of a contract where applicable, our legitimate interest in handling B2B enquiries and complying with legal obligations.

Sharing

Where necessary for a sourcing request, relevant information may be shared with prospective suppliers, logistics providers, professional advisers or service providers involved in operating our website and communication systems. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

We retain enquiry and transaction data only for as long as necessary for the business relationship, follow-up, legal obligations, accounting, dispute handling or other legitimate business purposes.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing. Contact us to exercise a right or ask a privacy question.
